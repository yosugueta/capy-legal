Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026 · capy.vision@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Capy ("Capy", "we", "us") is a mobile application developed by Yossi Gueta ("the Operator"), based in Rishon LeZion, Israel. Capy is an at-home visual-exercise companion designed to make daily vision-training routines more engaging.

Contact: capy.vision@gmail.com

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Capy iOS app and explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you and your child have. When the app is used by a child, a parent or legal guardian must create the account and accept this policy on the child's behalf.

4. The camera — processed on device, never uploaded

Capy may use the front camera to estimate the distance between the user's face and the screen so exercises calibrate correctly. Camera frames are processed entirely on the device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. No camera image, video, frame, or face data ever leaves the device. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is stored. Only a single number — the estimated distance in centimetres — is used in real time and then discarded.

5. Where data is stored

Account and exercise data are stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) under a Data Processing Addendum compliant with GDPR Article 28 and Standard Contractual Clauses. Israel benefits from an EU adequacy decision (renewed January 2024).

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted via Row-Level Security so each account can only see its own data.

7. Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (5741-1981, including Amendment 13 in force since 14 August 2025) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR, you may:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Export your data (JSON)
  • Delete the account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • File a complaint with the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or your local EU supervisory authority

To exercise any right, email capy.vision@gmail.com from the address used to register. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children's data — specific protections

Capy may be used by children under parental supervision. Accordingly:

  • Only a parent or legal guardian may create an account for a child.
  • We collect the minimum data needed to run the exercises.
  • We never show third-party ads or behavioural advertising.
  • There is no free-form chat between users. A display name is visible only to members of a club joined by an invite code, and any user can be blocked or reported from inside the app.
  • We never share a child's data with third parties for marketing.
  • A parent may delete the account at any time from Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us.

For users in the United States, we comply with COPPA: we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

9. Security

We follow the Israeli Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security) 5777-2017 at the medium security level. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production data is logged and audited. Crash data is anonymised.
